# 💎 Luna Crystals — Crystal Jewelry E-commerce

[🌐 Visit the website](https://luna-crystals.com/)

**Luna Crystals** is a fully responsive e-commerce platform focused on selling natural crystal jewelry. It was designed with performance, simplicity, and scalability in mind, transforming a previously manual business into a unified, streamlined digital experience.

---

## 🧠 Project Background

The client — a solo entrepreneur — was managing her entire sales process through spreadsheets and Instagram messages. She needed a more professional and organized way to showcase products, manage inventory, and handle customer interactions.

I proposed and developed a complete solution that included:

- A **modern and responsive online store** where customers can browse products, add items to the cart, and place orders.
- An **admin dashboard** where the business owner can easily add new products, track inventory, and manage orders.
- A **RESTful API** to handle product data, stock management, and user sessions.

This project was built from scratch and tailored to the real-world needs of a small business going digital for the first time.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **React.js** (with [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/))
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **Tailwind CSS** for styling
- **React Context API** + Custom Hooks
- **RESTful API** for full CRUD operations
